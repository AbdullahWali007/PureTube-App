# PureTube

### A cleaner, ad-free YouTube experience for Android.

PureTube is an Android application designed to provide a cleaner and more focused way to watch YouTube content without the constant interruption of advertisements.

The project was created independently with the goal of making video watching simpler, lighter, and more enjoyable.

🌐 **Website:** https://puretube.freedev.app/

---

## ✨ Features

* 🚫 **Ad-free video experience**
* 📺 **YouTube video browsing and playback**
* 🎬 **Full-screen video playback**
* 🔗 **Easy video sharing**
* 📱 **Android-native experience**
* ⚡ **Lightweight and responsive**
* 🎨 **Clean and simple interface**
* 🔒 **Privacy-focused**
* 🆓 **Free to use**

## ❤️ Why I Built PureTube

I built PureTube because I was tired of the increasingly distracting experience of watching videos online.

The idea is simple:

> **Open the app. Find what you want to watch. Enjoy it without unnecessary interruptions.**

PureTube is a free independent project created with the goal of helping people enjoy a cleaner video-watching experience.
